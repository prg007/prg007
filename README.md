### Building [Onlure](https://onlure.ca)

Ad platforms count views and clicks. The trail goes cold when someone walks into a store days later, pays at the register, checks out online or installs the app. [Onlure](https://onlure.ca) picks it back up for local businesses and brands: it connects the campaigns they run, with creators, flyers, static ads, Meta or Google ads, an online store or an app, to verified in-store visits, purchases and in-app conversions, and credits each one to the campaign that sent it. Based in Toronto.

- For brands: [onlure.ca/brands](https://onlure.ca/brands)
- For creators: [onlure.ca/creators](https://onlure.ca/creators)
- In ChatGPT and Claude: [onlure.ca/docs/ai-assistants](https://onlure.ca/docs/ai-assistants)
